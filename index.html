Meta Ads Best Practices
1
Prioritize creative testing over audience testing — creatives win the auction.
Refresh every 2–4 weeks to avoid fatigue
2
Use Conversion API (CAPI) wherever possible — better signal, better optimization.
3
Focus on conversion events, not vanity metrics like reach or impressions.
4
Avoid frequent budget changes — protect the learning phase.
Scale by max 15–20% every 3–4 days
5
Use broad targeting when you have 500+ monthly conversion events.
6
Build strong retargeting funnels — warm audiences convert 3–5× cheaper.

Meta Ads — Win / Loss Framework

Know exactly when to scale, fix, or cut campaigns

✓ Win — Scale it
  • Target CPA achieved
  • ROAS above goal
  • Stable conversion volume
  • Frequency under 3.0
⚡ Needs Optimization
  • Good CTR, weak conversions
  • Rising CPA trend
  • Declining ROAS week-on-week
  • High frequency (>3.5)
✗ Pause & Review
  • Multiple creatives tested
  • Multiple audiences tried
  • Different offers A/B tested
  • LP improved significantly
  • Still no target KPIs hit
Optimization Actions by Scenario
→
Good engagement, no conversions → Fix landing page or offer. LP CVR <1% is the problem.
→
Rising CPA → Refresh creatives, expand audience, check frequency.
→
Low CTR (<1%) → Hook and creative are the issue. Test 5+ new hooks.
→
High CPM → Audience too narrow or Q4 competition. Broaden or wait.
→
Low ATC rate (<5%) → Offer or product-market fit issue, not ads.

Competitor Research — SOP

Step-by-step process to track, analyze, and outperform competitors on Meta Ads

📌 Phase 1 — Identify Competitors
1
Search your product name in the target market region
Use Google, Instagram, and Facebook search. Note every brand that appears for the same keywords or in the same category.
2
Check how they are selling
Visit their website — note pricing, offers, bundles, guarantees, and checkout flow. Identify their main value proposition.
3
Check their social media presence
Review their Facebook Page and Instagram — follower count, post frequency, engagement rate, content style, and comments from customers.
🔍 Phase 2 — Ads Library Check
4
Go to Meta Ads Library → search competitor brand name
URL: facebook.com/ads/library — filter by Country = target market, Category = All Ads.
5
Check if they are running ads
If ads are showing → they are actively investing. If no ads → either organic only, different platform, or not yet advertising in this region.
6
If YES — list them as active competitor
Record: brand name, page URL, number of active ads, ad formats used (video/image/carousel), and approximate ad start dates.
💡 Tip: Sort by "Active ads" and look at who has been running the same ad for 30+ days — that ad is likely profitable for them. It's a strong signal to study it closely.
🏆 Phase 3 — Follow the Best Competitor
7
Identify the competitor doing it best
Criteria: most active ads, longest-running creatives, strong social engagement, professional landing page, clear offer structure.
8
Study their best practices — in detail
Note: hook style (first 3 seconds), ad copy structure, CTA used, offer type (discount/free shipping/bundle), landing page design, and headline messaging.
9
Do NOT copy — extract the winning pattern
Understand WHY it works: is it the urgency? The social proof? The visual? Apply the same principle with your own unique angle and offer.
📅 Phase 4 — Weekly Monitoring (Ongoing SOP)
W1
Every week — revisit Ads Library for top 3 competitors
Note any new ads launched, old ads paused, and any changes in messaging or creative format.
W2
Track what new creatives they are testing
If a competitor launches 5+ new ads in one week — they are in heavy testing mode. Pay close attention to which ones survive into the next week.
W3
Check social for new organic content and engagement
New product launches, promotions, or viral content can signal what is working for them — even before it becomes an ad.
W4
Log findings in your Competitor Tracker sheet
Record: date, competitor, new ad format, offer/hook used, estimated run length, and your action item in response.
What to Look for in Their Ads
  • Hook — first 3 seconds of video
  • Offer type — discount, bundle, free trial
  • Ad format — video, image, carousel
  • Primary text — problem vs benefit angle
  • CTA button — Shop Now, Learn More, etc.
  • Landing page — where the ad takes them
  • Social proof — reviews, UGC, testimonials
  • Ad longevity — running 30+ days = winner
Status Classification
A
Active — Running ads
Monitor weekly. Study winning creatives. Find gaps in their offer.
B
Inactive — No current ads
Check monthly. May re-enter. Opportunity to capture their audience now.
C
Not advertising at all
Low threat on paid. Focus on outcompeting organically or capture their search traffic via Google.
📌 Rule: Always check Ads Library before launching a new creative direction. If your top 3 competitors are all running UGC videos with a pain-point hook — that format is working in your market. Start there, then differentiate your angle and offer.

Match Type Strategy
Exact Match
[keyword]
Max control, lower volume. Core money keywords.
Phrase Match
"keyword"
Balanced control + reach. Mid-funnel terms.
Broad Match
keyword
Max reach. Only use with Smart Bidding + strong negatives.
Negative Keywords — Mandatory Expansion
1
Review Search Terms Report every week — non-negotiable.
Filter for zero-conversion terms spending budget
2
Add irrelevant terms as negatives at campaign or ad group level.
3
Common negatives to start: free, DIY, jobs, salary, course, tutorial, Reddit, YouTube.
4
Use Negative Keyword Lists at account level for global exclusions.

Google Ads — Win / Loss Framework

Decision tree for every campaign performance scenario

✓ Win — Scale it
  • CPA below target
  • ROAS above goal
  • Conversion volume growing
  • Quality Scores 7+
⚡ Needs Optimization
  • Strong traffic, weak CVR
  • Low CTR on search ads
  • High CPC draining budget
  • Low Quality Scores (<5)
✗ Stop & Review
  • Keywords optimized fully
  • Ads A/B tested
  • LP improved significantly
  • Search terms refined
  • Still no target KPIs
Optimization Actions by Scenario
→
Good traffic, no conversions → Check LP speed, form, message match, and CTA clarity.
→
Low CTR (<3%) → Rewrite headlines, add keyword to headline 1, test different value props.
→
High CPC → Improve Quality Score, tighten match types, add more negatives.
→
Low IS (<40%) → Increase budget or improve QS to unlock more eligible impressions.
→
Irrelevant search terms → Aggressive negative keyword expansion + tighten to phrase/exact match.
